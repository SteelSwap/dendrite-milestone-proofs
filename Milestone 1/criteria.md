## Important Notes

**Contingency Planning:**  
Due to the dependency on third parties, and aligned with our proposal, we reserve the right to remove integration partners and swap them with different or new partners. All project decisions will be included in the **Technical Project Report** asset.

**External Dependency Handling:**  
In the event that any integration partners are unreachable/no longer exist at the time of delivery/or are uncooperative with us, we will replace them with suitable alternatives so the total is 6. If there are no suitable alternatives to meet the 6 target (e.g., black swan event DJED is gone and there is no algorithmic stablecoin), we will provide details and a plan to replace them with another DEX, lending protocol, etc. to target at least 6 integrations through the project. This is to protect project delays in the event of external dependencies.

**Exceeding Minimum Requirements:**  
The team will go above and beyond the target 6 if at the time of delivery new projects exist that we think would be a good value to add (although this is optional).
